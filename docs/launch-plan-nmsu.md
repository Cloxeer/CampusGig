# NMSU Launch Plan — Liquidity & Brand (One Campus)

**GetCampusGig** · https://getcampusgig.com  
**Bottleneck:** Marketplace liquidity and brand on **one campus** (NMSU), not cloud infrastructure.  
**You can start this plan today** — summer is for planting; fall is for harvesting.

---

## How to use this doc

1. Read **North star** and **Metrics** once.
2. Do **Summer (now)** in order — posters, seed gigs, ambassadors.
3. When classes start, switch to **Fall semester** (tabling, clubs, weekly rhythm).
4. Treat **Sponsorships** as Phase 3 — only after you have active users.

Check boxes as you go. Copy/paste the scripts; adjust tone to sound like you.

---

## North star

| Priority | What it means |
|----------|----------------|
| **1. Liquidity** | Enough **open gigs** and **workers** that someone posts → gets a request → completes within a few days. |
| **2. Brand** | NMSU students say: *“Use GetCampusGig — it’s .edu only and safer than GroupMe.”* |
| **3. Trust** | Real completions → Karma Kredit → word of mouth. |

**Not a priority yet:** second campus, paid ads, rebuilding the database, sponsorship tab in the app.

---

## Metrics that matter (ignore vanity)

Track weekly in a simple spreadsheet or Notes app:

| Metric | Target (early) | Why |
|--------|----------------|-----|
| **Completed gigs / week** | 5+ by mid-fall | Proof the marketplace works |
| **Active posters** (posted ≥1 gig in 14 days) | 10+ | Supply |
| **Active workers** (requested or completed in 14 days) | 20+ | Demand |
| **Repeat users** (2+ actions ever) | Growing | Retention |
| Signups | Nice to have | Meaningless if nobody posts |

**One campus rule:** Do not expand to UNM/UTEP until NMSU hits **~30 completed gigs total** and **5+ completions/week** for two weeks straight.

## Summer vs fall — different jobs

| Season | Campus reality | Your job |
|--------|----------------|----------|
| **Summer** | Low foot traffic; many students gone | Posters, QR, seed **fake-but-real** gigs with friends, build waitlist, find 3–5 ambassadors for fall |
| **Fall** | High density; clubs, events, tabling | Booth, class shoutouts, club partnerships, push completions every week |

Summer marketing is **awareness + “save this for August.”**  
Fall marketing is **“post a gig today.”**

## Phase 1 — Summer (start now)

### Week 1: Make the poster + link works

**Goal:** One sharp flyer you can print 30–50 times.

#### Poster content (copy this)

**Headline (large):**  
`NMSU students — get paid for campus gigs`

**Subhead:**  
`Moving help · tutoring · errands · rides`  
`Only @nmsu.edu · contact shared after you're accepted`

**QR label:**  
`Scan to sign up — getcampusgig.com`

**Footer (small):**  
`Built by NMSU students · Las Cruces`

#### Design tips (Canva, Figma, or PowerPoint)

- Size: **8.5×11"** or **half-sheet (5.5×8.5")** — half-sheets are cheaper and fine for doors.
- Colors: charcoal text, white/off-white background, **green** for headline or QR border (match app).
- QR: generate at https://getcampusgig.com (or a short link if you use one later).
- Test: scan from 3 feet away before printing.

#### Before you print

- [ ] Sign up yourself with `@nmsu.edu` on production (not localhost).
- [ ] Post **one real test gig** and complete the flow with a friend.
- [ ] Fix any broken step — posters amplify bugs.

---

### Week 2: Dorm & high-traffic flyering

**Goal:** 30–50 posters placed where students actually look.

#### Where to put posters (summer)

| Location | Notes |
|----------|--------|
| Dorm **main entrances** & elevator lobbies | High visibility; check housing rules below |
| **Corbett / Garcia / Rhodes** common areas | If allowed |
| **Student Union** bulletin boards | Often official board — use proper pins/tape |
| **Library** entrance (if permitted) | Ask desk staff |
| **Zuhl / Engineering** building boards | CS/engineering early adopters |
| **Apartments** near campus (not just dorms) | International students, grad students |

#### Housing / rules (important)

- Many dorms require **RA or housing approval** for non-university flyers.
- **Ask first** when possible: *“I’m a student promoting a free campus gig app for NMSU emails only — can I put a few posters on the bulletin board?”*
- Use **removable tape**; don’t damage paint.
- If told no → move to next building; don’t debate.

#### Summer script (if someone asks)

> “It’s GetCampusGig — like TaskRabbit but only for NMSU students. You sign up with your school email, post a gig or request one, and contact info only shows after the poster accepts you. We’re getting ready for fall.”

### Week 3: Seed the marketplace (critical)

An empty app kills word of mouth. **You must fake liquidity ethically** — real tasks, real money or favors, real completion.

#### Seed gig ideas (post 10–15 over 2 weeks)

| Gig | Pay tag idea | Who does it |
|-----|--------------|-------------|
| Help move mini-fridge / boxes | Cash / Venmo | You + friends |
| Pick up textbook from bookstore | Small cash | Friend |
| 1-hour tutoring (calc, CS) | Cash or trade | Friend who tutors |
| Ride to airport / Walmart | Gas money | Friend with car |
| Take photos for LinkedIn headshot | Free coffee | You |
| Dog walk / plant water while away | $10–20 | Neighbor student |

#### Rules for seed gigs

- **Real work, real completion** — run the full flow (post → request → accept → complete → verify → Karma).
- Vary categories so Explore doesn’t look fake.
- After fall starts, **stop seeding** once organic gigs appear.

### Week 4: Ambassadors + fall waitlist

**Goal:** 3–5 people who will blast the app the first week of classes.

#### Who to recruit

- One person per **large dorm**
- One **club officer** (entrepreneurship, CS, business)
- One **international student org** contact
- One **Greek life** or intramurals person (optional)

#### Ambassador offer (simple)

> “You’re an early ambassador for GetCampusGig. Before classes start: post 1 gig, share the link in your group chat once, and put 2 posters in your dorm. First week of school I’ll buy you coffee and you get ‘founding member’ on your profile if we add that later.”

#### Give ambassadors

- [ ] Link: https://getcampusgig.com  
- [ ] 5 half-sheet posters (PDF you send)  
- [ ] 3-line script for GroupMe / Discord / Snapchat  

**GroupMe script:**

> yo — NMSU-only gig app for random campus jobs (moving, tutoring, errands). .edu sign in only. getcampusgig.com — I’m using it for [specific gig], lmk if you want in

## Phase 2 — Fall semester (liquidity push)

### First 2 weeks of classes — “Launch fortnight”

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Mon | Ambassadors post 1 gig each; you post 2 |
| Tue | Table at Student Union (see tabling section) |
| Wed | Visit 2 club meetings (ask 2 min at end) |
| Thu | Instagram story: screenshot of completed gig + Karma |
| Fri | Remind everyone: “post before the weekend” |
| Weekend | Monitor requests; accept fast (speed = trust) |

Repeat until **5+ completions/week**.

---

### Tabling / booth — step by step (never done this before)

#### What a table is

A physical spot where you stand with a laptop or phone, QR code on a standee, and explain the app in 30 seconds. Goal: **signups + first gig posted on the spot**, not flyers only.

#### How to get permission (NMSU)

1. **Student Union / ASNMSU** — search “NMSU student organization tabling” or “ASNMSU events.”  
   - Register as a **student org** if required, or partner with an existing org (CS club, ENACTUS, etc.).
2. **Department tables** — Engineering, Business — ask advisor: *“Can I demo a student project at the career fair / welcome week?”*
3. **Club fair / OrgFest** — usually first weeks of semester; apply early (often July/August for fall).

**If you can’t get official table space:** “Pop-up” tabling with a friend + QR standee **on public Union walkways** may still need permission — always ask Union staff first.

#### What to bring

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Laptop or tablet | Live demo signup |
| Large QR print (letter or tabloid) | Scan from distance |
| 20 half-sheet flyers | Handout |
| Snacks (optional) | Stops people — “free candy, 30 sec pitch” |
| Sign: `NMSU ONLY · @nmsu.edu` | Trust in one line |

#### 30-second pitch (memorize)

> “GetCampusGig — campus gigs only for NMSU students. Sign in with your school email, post something you need done or pick up a gig. Venmo-style pay tags. Contact info only unlocks after the poster accepts you, so it’s not random DMs.”

#### Ask every person who stops

1. “Do you have `@nmsu.edu`?” → if no, politely skip.  
2. “Want to post a gig or browse?” → open site on **their** phone if possible.  
3. “What’s one thing you need done this week?” → help them post it on the spot.

#### Table success metric

- **10 signups** = okay day  
- **3 gigs posted at the table** = great day  
- **1 completion within 7 days** = you’re winning

## Phase 3 — Sponsorships (later, not now)

**Do not build a Sponsorships tab until:**

- [ ] **100+ signups** with `@nmsu.edu`, AND  
- [ ] **30+ completed gigs**, AND  
- [ ] You can say: *“We had X active students last month.”*

Until then, sponsors have no audience. Liquidity first.

### What the tab could be (future product)

- **“Deals”** tab: local businesses offer a discount if user shows app profile or completed-gig badge.
- You do **not** process payments — businesses honor codes themselves.
- Start with **3–5 Las Cruces spots** students already love (coffee, pizza, boba, barber near campus).

### How to pitch a local business (phone or in person)

> “I’m a student at NMSU. I built an app where only NMSU students can post and pick up campus gigs — we have [NUMBER] students signed up. I’m looking for one local partner to offer a small discount — like 10% off — for students who show the app. You’d get listed in the app and on our Instagram. No upfront fee for a pilot.”

**What you offer them**

- Logo + name in app (future tab)
- Instagram shoutout when you post
- Optional: “first gig free” promo tied to their shop

**What you ask**

- 10–15% discount OR free drink with purchase
- Permission to use their logo
- 3-month pilot, no contract

**What to avoid early**

- Complex coupon codes, payment splits, or % of student transactions
- Chains that need corporate approval — start **local owner-operated**

## When things feel stuck

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| People sign up but don’t post | Ask “what do you need done this week?” and post **for** them at a table |
| Gigs sit with no requests | Share gig link in GroupMe; lower pay friction; seed a request from a friend |
| Posters ignored | Move to tabling + in-person; summer dorms are empty |
| “Is this safe?” | Emphasize `.edu only` + contact after accept + Karma rep |
| No money for sponsors | Don’t — liquidity first |
